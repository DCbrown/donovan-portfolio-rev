The path here

Every step taught me something about the people on the other side of the screen.

  1. 2010 to 2014

    Help desk and field service

    Hewlett Packard, then Henry Ford OptimEyes, then Detroit Medical Center. Learned to fix the problem and explain it without making anyone feel small.

  2. 2015 onward

    Self-taught developer

    freeCodeCamp, Grand Circus, and a lot of late nights. Shipped client sites, a progressive web app, and an iOS app. Developer at Detroit Labs, then UI developer at CQL.

  3. 2017 onward

    Instructor and mentor

    Lead instructor with JOURNi. Speaker at Black Tech Saturdays and Per Scholas. Founded Code Connect Mentoring in 2024 for career-changers.

  4. Now

    Applied AI

    Setting up Claude, Cowork, and automation workflows for small businesses. Building AI applications with production instincts. Teaching it all on AI And Tech With Donovan and with AI Collective Detroit.
